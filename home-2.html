Fleet tyre health overview

Turn tyre condition into a maintenance priority list.

Instead of treating each vehicle as a surprise visit, build a repeatable view of pressure, tread, damage and wear so urgent work is separated from planned work.

01

Tread monitoring

Record usable tread and flag vehicles approaching planned replacement thresholds.

02

Pressure control

Cold-pressure and TPMS checks reduce avoidable under-inflation and abnormal wear.

03

Damage inspection

Separate repairable punctures from casing, shoulder or sidewall damage that needs replacement.

04

Wear-pattern tracking

Use inner/outer wear and steering symptoms to trigger alignment or suspension investigation.

Duty-cycle intelligence

The route changes what the tyre needs.

Tyre pressure, wear rate and maintenance timing should reflect operating pattern rather than one fixed schedule for every vehicle.

Urban delivery

Stops, kerbs & tight routes

More frequent visual checks and pressure control help catch impact damage and shoulder wear early.

Highway logistics

Heat, mileage & uptime

Pressure consistency, tread planning and scheduled rotation become higher priorities at sustained speed.

Taxi / passenger

High daily utilisation

Predictable inspection intervals help protect grip without pulling revenue vehicles off the road unnecessarily.

Mixed commercial

Different jobs, one programme

Segment vehicles by duty instead of forcing vans, pool cars and service vehicles into one tyre cycle.

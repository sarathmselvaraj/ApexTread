Tyres service

What this service covers

A good tyre change is more than removing one tyre and fitting another. We confirm the tyre size and specification, inspect the wheel and valve area, mount the replacement with alloy-safe equipment, set pressure to the vehicle recommendation and complete the final torque check. If we notice rim, valve or tyre-condition concerns, we explain them before any extra work is added.

Inside the bay

What happens in the bay

Every step is explained clearly, with extra work approved before it is added.

01

Confirm fitment

We verify tyre size, load/speed rating and direction or asymmetric markings before the old tyre comes off.

02

Fit & balance

The tyre is mounted with wheel-protection procedures, inflated correctly and balanced where required.

03

Final safety check

Wheel fasteners, pressure, valve condition and visual seating are checked before handover.

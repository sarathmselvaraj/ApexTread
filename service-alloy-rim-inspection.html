Alloy & Rim Inspection

A focused rim, bead-seat and wheel-face inspection to identify impact damage, sealing concerns and wheel-related vibration risks before tyre work.

← All services
Repair service

What this service covers

A tyre can only seal and run correctly when the wheel supporting it is in suitable condition. Kerb strikes and potholes can damage the rim edge, bead seat or wheel shape. We inspect visible impact areas, look for corrosion or sealing concerns and consider whether wheel condition could be contributing to pressure loss or vibration. This is an inspection service; structural repair or replacement is quoted separately if required.

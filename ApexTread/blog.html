Quick reference

Before your next long drive

Five simple checks can catch the most common tyre problems before they interrupt the journey.

  • Check cold tyre pressures against the vehicle placard.
  • Inspect tread across the full width, not just the centre.
  • Look for cuts, bulges, nails and fresh impact marks.
  • Notice steering pull or vibration before motorway speed.
  • Confirm the spare or mobility kit is usable.
